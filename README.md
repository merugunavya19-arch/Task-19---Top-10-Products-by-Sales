# Task 19 – Top 10 Products by Sales

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Sample Superstore dataset** to identify and rank the top 10 products based on total sales. The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Excel and Python** to summarize product sales, practice ranking and sorting, and create visual comparisons.

## Objective

- Identify the top 10 products based on total sales.
- Calculate total sales for each product.
- Rank products based on their sales performance.
- Compare sales among the top-performing products.
- Identify the highest-selling product.
- Create visualizations to present the top 10 products.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample - Superstore.xlsx

- Rows: 9,994
- Columns: 21
- Sheet: `RawData`
- Product Name Column: `Q`
- Sales Column: `R`

## Methodology

1. Loaded the Sample Superstore dataset.
2. Selected the **Product Name** and **Sales** columns.
3. Created a PivotTable to summarize sales by product.
4. Calculated total sales for each product using **Sum of Sales**.
5. Sorted the products in descending order of total sales.
6. Applied a **Top 10 Value Filter** to identify the 10 highest-selling products.
7. Checked the ranking and possible ties in sales values.
8. Created a bar chart to visualize the top 10 products by sales.
9. Used Python and Pandas to group and sort product sales.
10. Created a visualization to compare the sales performance of the top 10 products.
